🚀 **I built something to fix the biggest problem in coding: Inconsistency.**

We’ve all been there—starting a tutorial with high energy, only to drop off three days later because life got in the way.

So, I decided to build a solution.

Meet **PyDaily** 🐍

It’s an **Infinite Python Bootcamp** that runs entirely on autopilot. 

Instead of a static course, I built a system that generates a unique, bite-sized lesson and delivers it **straight to your inbox every single morning**. No friction, no login walls—just pure Python knowledge served with your morning coffee. ☕

It doesn't just teach you syntax; it adapts to your progress, throwing "Senior Dev" level challenges (we call them Boss Battles) your way when you're ready to level up. 

**Under the hood:**
I built this using **Python, Streamlit, and Supabase**, with **Google Gemini 1.5 Pro** powering the creative engine. The secret sauce? I pair-programmed the entire architecture with an advanced AI agent (Antigravity), turning a complex idea into a shipped product in record time.
